SAE Rear Wing Mount

Project and Highlights

Designed a rear wing mounting assembly for the Ignite design challenge. The goal was to build a strong, lightweight mount that could handle all the target load requirements given in the competition criteria without flexing or failing.

  • Simulated Under Load (FEA): Ran stress simulations in SolidWorks to make sure the mount wouldn't bend or break under the challenge's force requirements.
  • Lightweighted: Cut away unnecessary material with pocketing to keep the mount as light as possible while keeping it strong.
  • Reinforced High-Stress Areas: Added smooth fillets and support ribs around bolt holes to prevent cracks under load.
  • Custom Brackets: Brackets was flush to the chassis to provide high strength

What I Learned

First time using Solidworks! Geometry Dictates How Parts React Under Load

  • How Different Designs Handle Stress: I got to see firsthand how changing the shape of the mount completely shifted where the stress concentrated under different load conditions.
  • Downforce vs. Side Loads: Comparing different models showed me that a design that held up great under downward force might flex or twist when hit with a side load, requiring a smarter structural layout rather than just adding thicker material.
  • Iterating for the Right Balance: Testing multiple versions taught me that design is a process—iterating through different concepts helped me find the right balance between low weight and maximum strength.
